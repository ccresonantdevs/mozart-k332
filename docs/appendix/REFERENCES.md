## 實際檢驗之現代學術分析

- **Beach, David**. “The Initial Movements of Mozart’s Piano Sonatas K. 280 and K. 332: Some Striking Similarities.” *Intégral* 8 (1994): 125–146.
- **Hatten, Robert S.** “On Musical Topics and Tropes. Definitions, History, Theoretical Amplifications, Pedagogical Applications, and Extensions.” *Res Facta Nova* 24 (33) (2023): 9–22. DOI: 10.14746/rfn.2023.24.1.
- **Hood, Danielle Kathleen**. *Doubles and Duplicity: Topics in Vienna Around the Long Fin-de-Siècle, 1874–1928*. PhD diss., University of Leeds, 2015.
- **McKay, Nicholas**. “On Topics Today.” *Zeitschrift der Gesellschaft für Musiktheorie* 4/1–2 (2007): 159–183. DOI: 10.31751/251.
- **Rabinovitch, Gilad**. “Reimagining Historical Improvisation: Robert Levin and the Case of Mozart.” *Music Theory Online* 26, no. 2 (2020).
- **Ross, Clark**. “Structural Analysis of Mozart: Piano Sonata in F Major (K. 332); I.” Music 3107 course analysis, Memorial University of Newfoundland (Fall 2012).
- **Sanguinetti, Giorgio**. “Mozart, il pianista e il teatro dei segni.” In *Interpretare Mozart: Atti del Convegno*, edited by Mariateresa Dellaborra, Guido Salvetti, and Claudio Toscani, pp. 3–19. Lucca: Libreria Musicale Italiana, 2007. ISBN 978-88-7096-503-2.
- **Silbiger, Alexander**. “'Il chitarrino le suonerò': Commedia dell’arte in Mozart’s Piano Sonata K. 332.” Paper presented to the Mozart Society of America (1999) and the Southeast Chapter of the American Musicological Society (2000), revised 2010.
